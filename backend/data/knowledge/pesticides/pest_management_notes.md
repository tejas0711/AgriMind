# Pest Management - General Notes (Reference Only)

This system does not generate specific pesticide names, dosages, or mixing instructions from
general knowledge. Any pest/pesticide guidance surfaced by AgriMind must be grounded in
authoritative, current sources (e.g. state agricultural department advisories, ICAR/CIBRC
approved product lists) which should be indexed here as they become available. Until such
authoritative documents are indexed, the system should respond to pesticide questions by stating
this limitation rather than guessing.

General principles that ARE safe to state without specific product data:
- Integrated Pest Management (IPM) favors monitoring and cultural/biological controls first.
- Chemical control should be a last resort and match the specific pest and crop stage.
- Pre-harvest intervals and label instructions on the actual product packaging always take
  precedence over generic advice.
