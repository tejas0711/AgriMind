# 4R Nutrient Stewardship and Fertilizer Management Guidelines

Source: Fertilizer Association of India (FAI). *Handbook on Fertilizer Technology and 4R Nutrient Stewardship.* New Delhi.

## The 4R Nutrient Stewardship Framework

The 4R framework ensures that fertilizer applications achieve balanced crop nutrition while minimizing losses to water and the atmosphere:

1. **Right Source:** Match fertilizer type to soil properties and crop needs (e.g. neem-coated urea for nitrogen, single superphosphate to supply both phosphorus and sulphur).
2. **Right Rate:** Match amount of fertilizer to crop nutrient demand based on soil test calibration rather than habit.
3. **Right Time:** Synchronize nutrient application with crop uptake peaks; avoid applying on saturated or high-runoff soils.
4. **Right Place:** Place nutrients where plant roots can easily access them while minimizing surface exposure and leaching.

## Placement and Timing Rules for Major Nutrients

### Nitrogen Management
- Nitrogen is vulnerable to ammonia volatilization under hot dry conditions and leaching under waterlogged conditions.
- **Split Applications:** Never apply total nitrogen at once. Divide into 2–3 splits (e.g. 50% basal or at tillering/early vegetative, 25% at active vegetative stage, 25% at panicle initiation/flowering).
- **Neem Coating:** Neem-coated urea retards nitrification, slowing down release and increasing nitrogen use efficiency by 10–15%.
- **Weather Sensitivity:** Do not broadcast urea if heavy rain (>20 mm) is imminent (prevents surface runoff) or if wind speed exceeds 30 km/h (prevents uneven distribution).

### Phosphorus Management
- Phosphorus is relatively immobile and strongly bound to soil particles.
- **Basal Placement:** Apply 100% of recommended phosphorus at or immediately before sowing/planting.
- **Root Zone Placement:** Place DAP or SSP 3–5 cm below and beside the seed row to maximize contact with developing roots. Surface broadcasting of phosphorus is inefficient and wasteful.

### Potassium Management
- Potassium regulates plant water retention, stomatal closure, enzyme activation, and lodging resistance.
- **Basal & Split Applications:** On medium to heavy textured soils (clays, vertisols), apply full dose as basal. On light sandy soils prone to leaching, split into two equal applications (basal + top-dressing).
- **Imbalance Warning:** When soil test potassium is HIGH (>280 kg/ha), additional potash fertilizers should not be applied because excessive potassium interferes with magnesium and calcium uptake.

## Weather and Environmental Considerations

- **Runoff Mitigation:** Do not apply chemical fertilizers or slurry to waterlogged fields, cracked dry vertisols before the first monsoon wetting, or during forecast storm events.
- **Volatilization Control:** High temperatures (>38°C) combined with high surface soil pH accelerate ammonia loss from urea. Incorporate fertilizer into soil or irrigate immediately following application.
- **Spray Drift:** Foliar micronutrient sprays must only be applied when wind speed is below 15–20 km/h and during early morning or late afternoon to avoid leaf scorch.
