# Organic Soil Amendment Practices

## Farmyard Manure (FYM)
Well-decomposed FYM applied at 5-10 tonnes per hectare improves both nutrient content and soil
structure. It should be applied 2-3 weeks before sowing so it has time to partially decompose in
the field rather than competing with the crop for nitrogen during decomposition.

## Green Manuring
Fast-growing legumes such as Dhaincha (Sesbania) or Sunhemp (Crotalaria) are grown for 45-60 days
and then ploughed into the soil before flowering. This adds nitrogen (through nitrogen fixation)
and organic matter, and is most effective when repeated across multiple seasons rather than once.

## Vermicompost
Vermicompost is nutrient-dense relative to raw FYM and also improves microbial activity in the
soil. It is commonly used at 2-3 tonnes per hectare as a basal application, often blended with
regular compost for larger fields to manage cost.

## Crop Residue Management
Retaining and incorporating crop residue (instead of burning it) returns organic carbon and
potassium to the soil over time, and is one of the lowest-cost ways to gradually raise a field's
organic carbon rating.
